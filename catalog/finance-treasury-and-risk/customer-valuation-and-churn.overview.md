# Customer Valuation and Churn Overview

This entry calculates customer lifetime profit margins and analyzes churn rates across active subscriptions. It is also known by its components, including "Customer Churn / Attrition" and "Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)", as well as their common aliases like "customer LTV" and "LTV".

## Key Concepts

### Customer Churn / Attrition
"Customer Churn" and "Attrition" are synonymous terms referring to the rate at which subscribers deactivate or leave their service over a specific period. This metric is tracked by care agents and analytics teams to help identify issues such as pricing problems, network performance gaps, or competitive pressure.

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)
Customer Lifetime Value, abbreviated as CLV or LTV, represents the total net profit expected to be earned from a customer over their entire relationship. It is calculated as projected revenue minus the cost to serve and retain them across their expected lifetime. While "customer LTV" and "LTV" are frequently used, particularly in marketing presentations, **CLV** is the designated authoritative term for official finance reporting.
