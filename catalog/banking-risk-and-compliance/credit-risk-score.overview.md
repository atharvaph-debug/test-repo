# Credit Risk Score Overview

A Credit Risk Score is a quantified estimate of a borrower's default likelihood, serving as a crucial piece of metadata in banking operations. It is instrumental in driving credit approvals, determining interest rates, and setting loan terms.

## Key Features

*   **Calculation Inputs**: The Credit Risk Score is calculated based on several key factors, including the borrower's repayment history, current exposure, and income/leverage.
*   **Operational Impact**: This score directly influences several critical financial decisions and processes:
    *   Credit lending approvals
    *   Triggers for manual underwriting
    *   Determination of financial terms such as interest rates, credit limits, and pricing.
*   **Metadata Distinction**: It is critical to recognize the Credit Risk Score as a distinct metadata attribute on a customer's profile, separate from the KYC Risk Rating. While the Credit Risk Score assesses default risk, the KYC Risk Rating measures potential financial-crime and money laundering risk. Confusing or conflating these two distinct metadata attributes is a common operational error.
