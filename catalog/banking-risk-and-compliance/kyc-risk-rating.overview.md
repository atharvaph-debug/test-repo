# KYC Risk Rating Overview

The KYC Risk Rating is an operational metadata tag assigned to customers during onboarding and refreshed periodically. It quantifies a customer's potential financial crime and money laundering risk.

## Key Features

*   **Categorization:** The KYC Risk Rating is categorized as either *Standard* or *Enhanced* due diligence, reflecting different levels of scrutiny.
*   **Purpose:** It measures the potential financial crime and money laundering risk associated with a customer.
*   **Lifecycle:** This rating is initially assigned during the customer onboarding process and is subsequently refreshed on a periodic basis to ensure its continued accuracy.
*   **Equivalence:** The KYC Risk Rating is equivalent to Customer Due Diligence (CDD).
*   **System Integration:** It serves as the baseline configuration for risk-based transaction monitoring systems, guiding how customer transactions are scrutinized for suspicious activity.
*   **Distinction from Credit Risk Score:** It is crucial to store and understand the KYC Risk Rating (focused on financial-crime risk) as distinct metadata from the Credit Risk Score (focused on default risk). Conflating these two can lead to operational errors.
