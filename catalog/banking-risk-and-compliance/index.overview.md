# Banking Risk and Compliance

Onboarding, AML financial crime compliance, and risk profiling standards for bank customers.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/banking-risk-and-compliance/credit-risk-score | Credit Risk Score | file | A quantified estimate of a borrower's default likelihood, driving credit approvals, interest rates, and loan terms. |
| catalog/banking-risk-and-compliance/kyc-risk-rating | KYC Risk Rating | file | An operational metadata tag assigned during onboarding that measures a customer's potential financial crime and money laundering risk. |
| catalog/banking-risk-and-compliance/suspicious-activity-report | Suspicious Activity Report | file | The formal, time-sensitive regulatory filing submitted when an investigator establishes a reasonable suspicion of illicit transaction activity. |
