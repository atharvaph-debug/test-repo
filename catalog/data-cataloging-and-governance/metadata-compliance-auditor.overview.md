# Metadata Compliance Auditor Overview

The Metadata Compliance Auditor (also known by aliases `mca` and `compliance-audit-agent`) is an automated auditing tool designed to continuously scan metadata repositories. Its primary function is to ensure that these repositories comply with both enterprise-specific standards and regulatory requirements. This tool falls under the category of Data Cataloging and Governance, emphasizing its role in maintaining data quality and adherence to defined policies within an organization's data landscape.
