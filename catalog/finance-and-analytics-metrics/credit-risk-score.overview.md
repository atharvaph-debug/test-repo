# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score is a financial projection that quantifies the probability of a borrower defaulting on a credit obligation. This metric is distinct from compliance-driven Know Your Customer (KYC) ratings, focusing purely on financial default risk.

## Definition and Purpose

The Credit Risk Score is a numerical projection that estimates the likelihood of a borrower failing to meet their credit obligations. It is derived from an analysis of historical repayment patterns, current outstanding exposures, and the borrower's income or leverage profiles.

## Applications

This score is a critical tool that informs lending decisions, helps in the accurate pricing of loans, and supports ongoing monitoring of portfolio risk. A lower Credit Risk Score indicates a higher probability of default.

## Distinction from KYC Risk Rating

It is important to differentiate the Credit Risk Score from the Know Your Customer (KYC) risk rating. While the Credit Risk Score assesses the probability of default, the KYC rating specifically evaluates risks related to financial crime and compliance.
