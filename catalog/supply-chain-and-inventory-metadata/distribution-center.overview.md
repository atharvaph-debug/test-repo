# Distribution Center Overview

A Distribution Center (DC), also known by the alias `dc`, is an operational logistics node that receives, stores, and fulfills order inventory between upstream suppliers and downstream demand. It falls under the `Supply Chain and Inventory Metadata` category.

## Definition and Role
A Distribution Center (DC) is defined as an operational facility that receives inventory in bulk from upstream suppliers, stores it, and fulfills downstream orders for stores or end customers ([Warehouse Operations Runbook](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8814594), [Logistics Network Overview](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/9011201)).

## Metadata and Operations
Within Distribution Centers, key metadata events are tracked at the SKU level. These tracked events include inbound receipts, storage tracking, and outbound shipping, which are crucial for operational visibility and management ([Warehouse Operations Runbook](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8814594)).

## Network Context
In the logistics network graph, each DC represents a physical node that is strategically positioned between suppliers (upstream) and customer demand (downstream) ([Warehouse Operations Runbook](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8814594), [Logistics Network Overview](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/9011201)).
