# Bill of Materials Overview

The Bill of Materials (BOM), also known by its alias BOM, provides the structured definition of a finished product's physical components, sub-assemblies, and exact quantities. It serves as the authoritative and structured definition of a physical product's composition.

## Definition & Structure

The BOM outlines a hierarchical structure for any finished-good Stock Keeping Unit (SKU), documenting every component, sub-assembly, and the precise quantities required to construct a single finished unit. This structured definition functions as the primary metadata translation layer, bridging engineering design intent with subsequent procurement activities.

## Integration & Use Cases

A Bill of Materials is central to product lifecycle management and supply chain operations. For all manufactured or assembled items, each finished-good SKU is programmatically linked to a multi-level BOM. In procurement, when a purchase requisition is generated for manufactured products, the planning system automatically "explodes" this requisition against the product’s BOM to precisely determine the required component parts.
