# Traffic Source Overview

Traffic Source refers to the marketing channel that acquired a customer. This concept is fundamental for understanding customer acquisition and performing channel attribution analysis.

## Key Features

*   **Definition:** The marketing channel responsible for acquiring a customer.
*   **Examples:** Common traffic sources include Search, Organic, Email, Display, and Facebook.
*   **Storage Location:** This information is stored in the `users.traffic_source` field within the data.
*   **Analytical Use:** Traffic Source forms the basis for analyzing customer acquisition strategies and attributing success to specific marketing channels.
