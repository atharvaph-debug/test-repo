# Product Taxonomy Overview

The Product Taxonomy defines the three-level hierarchy used to organize products: Department, Category, and Brand.

## Key Features

The taxonomy structures products into three distinct levels:
*   **Department:** The highest level of classification, with examples including "Men" and "Women."
*   **Category:** A mid-level classification within a Department, such as "Jeans" or "Outerwear."
*   **Brand:** The specific brand associated with the product.

This hierarchical organization is used to categorize and manage all products.
