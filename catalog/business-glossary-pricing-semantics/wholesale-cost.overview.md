# Wholesale Cost Overview

Wholesale Cost represents the fundamental cost of a product, specifically the wholesale or landed cost paid to the supplier. This financial metric is crucial for internal accounting and profitability analysis but is strictly restricted from customer-facing environments.

## Key Features

*   **Definition**: Wholesale Cost is the total cost incurred to acquire a product from a supplier, including the purchase price and any additional costs (such as shipping, customs duties, etc.) that bring the product to its destination, hence "landed cost."
*   **Confidentiality**: This cost is considered sensitive business information and is explicitly prohibited from being displayed or made accessible in any customer-facing views.
*   **Data Location**: The Wholesale Cost is recorded in internal systems within the fields `products.cost` and `inventory_items.cost`.

## Relationship to Other Pricing Concepts

Unlike the Retail Price, which is the standard advertised catalog price, or the Sale Price, which is the final transactional amount paid by a customer, the Wholesale Cost represents the internal acquisition cost and is not directly related to customer transactions or pricing strategies visible to the public.
