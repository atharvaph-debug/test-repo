# KYC Risk Rating Overview

The KYC Risk Rating is a classification that determines the level of scrutiny required for transaction monitoring to detect financial crimes. It serves as an internal assessment used to manage and mitigate financial crime risk.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Purpose:** The primary purpose of the KYC Risk Rating is to dictate the subsequent level of transaction monitoring scrutiny applied to a customer. This allows for active monitoring where the actual flow of funds is compared against the customer's profile to flag unusual activities, such as unexpected volumes or structuring.
*   **Rating Types:** KYC Risk Ratings are assigned as either "standard" or "enhanced."
*   **Relationship with KYC/CDD:** KYC (Know Your Customer) processes, which are considered equivalent to Customer Due Diligence (CDD), involve verifying applicant identities at onboarding and periodically refreshing customer profiles. The KYC Risk Rating is an output of these processes.
*   **Distinction from Credit Risk Score:** It is crucial to maintain a structural distinction between the KYC Risk Rating and a Credit Risk Score. While a Credit Risk Score quantifies a borrower's default risk based on factors like repayment history and income, the KYC Risk Rating specifically measures financial crime risk and does not calculate default risk.
