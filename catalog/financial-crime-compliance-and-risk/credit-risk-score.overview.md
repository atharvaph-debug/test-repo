# Credit Risk Score Overview

The **Credit Risk Score**, also known as `credit-risk-rating`, is a quantitative estimate used in Financial Crime Compliance and Risk to assess a borrower's default risk.

## Definition and Purpose

A Credit Risk Score is a quantified estimate of a borrower's default risk. It is derived from several key factors, including repayment history, current exposure, income, and leverage. This score plays a crucial role in enterprise risk frameworks by quantifying credit risk at the borrower level. It is used to determine loan pricing at origination and to monitor portfolio default risk on an ongoing basis, which informs expected-loss provisioning.

## Key Distinctions

It is crucial to understand that the Credit Risk Score must be kept structurally distinct from the KYC (Know Your Customer) risk rating. While both are risk assessments, the Credit Risk Score specifically measures *default risk*, whereas the KYC risk rating is designed to measure *financial-crime risk* and does not calculate default risk.
