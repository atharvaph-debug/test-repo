# Number Portability Overview

Number Portability refers to the process and regulatory capability that allows a customer to retain their existing phone number when changing telecommunications carriers. This concept is central to metadata enrichment efforts, as standardizing its terminology and clarifying operational parameters across systems resolves ambiguity, prevents compliance failures, and enhances cross-functional reporting accuracy.

## Terminology

"Number portability" is a broad term that is synonymous with "Local Number Portability" (LNP), "Mobile Number Portability" (MNP), and "porting" [Copy of Customer Care Handbook](1mGNA1aQHvYEJS6N1cmr5N3nkTkZ8fX7Hjz-IvFOd_3o), [Number Portability Process SOP](1iX1WcU06wst5e-qRfVN6g8F12Mp-lICKmEfEJPs33I0). All these terms describe the same fundamental ability for a customer to keep their phone number when switching service providers.

## Key Procedures

The number portability process involves distinct procedures for both gaining and losing carriers:

*   **Port-In**: This procedure describes the actions taken by the *gaining* carrier. They are responsible for collecting the customer's current account details and PINs, and subsequently submitting this information to a central porting clearinghouse. A common challenge during the validation window is data mismatches, which are the primary cause of rejection. Once validated, the number is successfully cut over to the new carrier [Number Portability Process SOP](1iX1WcU06wst5e-qRfVN6g8F12Mp-lICKmEfEJPs33I0).
*   **Port-Out**: This procedure outlines the obligations of the *losing* carrier. They must validate port-out requests promptly and release the number within specific timelines mandated by the regulator. Delays or outright blocks of porting requests are prohibited. While prohibited from blocking, carriers may present retention offers to the customer before the number is released. Any stalled port-out processes are to be escalated to the interconnect team [Number Portability Process SOP](1iX1WcU06wst5e-qRfVN6g8F12Mp-lICKmEfEJPs33I0).
