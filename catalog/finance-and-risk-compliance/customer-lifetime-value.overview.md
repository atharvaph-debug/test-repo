# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total net profit expected to be earned from a customer over their entire operational relationship with the company. This metric is crucial for understanding the long-term value of customer relationships.

## Definition and Calculation

Customer Lifetime Value is defined as the total net profit expected to be earned from a customer over their entire relationship. It is calculated as projected revenue minus the cost to serve and retain them, summed across their expected lifetime.

## Synonyms and Usage

"Customer LTV" and "LTV" are informal synonyms commonly used in Marketing slide decks. While these terms refer to the same metric, "CLV" is standardized in Finance reporting. All three terms must be mapped to a single metadata concept to facilitate reconciliation between marketing decks and finance dashboards.

## Optimization Drivers

Customer Lifetime Value can be improved by:
*   Lengthening the customer relationship (Retention).
*   Raising periodic revenue per customer (Cross-sell).
