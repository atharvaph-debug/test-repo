# Finance and Risk Compliance

Core metrics, risk scoring systems, compliance models, and processing runbooks used within corporate treasury and retail banking operations.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/finance-and-risk-compliance/credit-risk-score | Credit Risk Score | file | A calculated estimate of borrower default risk generated from historical repayment schedules, leverage ratios, and outstanding exposure metrics. |
| catalog/finance-and-risk-compliance/customer-lifetime-value | Customer Lifetime Value | file | The total net profit expected to be earned from a customer over their entire operational relationship with the company. |
| catalog/finance-and-risk-compliance/liquidity-coverage-ratio | Liquidity Coverage Ratio | file | The ratio of high-quality liquid assets to projected net cash outflows over a 30-day stress window managed under Basel III standards. |
| catalog/finance-and-risk-compliance/net-interest-margin | Net Interest Margin | file | A balance-sheet profitability metric calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets. |
