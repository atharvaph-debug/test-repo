# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM), also known by its alias NIM, is a crucial balance-sheet profitability metric within Finance and Risk Compliance. It quantifies how efficiently a company transforms its balance sheet into net interest income.

## Key Features

*   **Definition**: Net Interest Margin is a balance-sheet profitability metric calculated as net interest income (interest earned on assets minus payments made on liabilities) divided by average interest-earning assets. It represents the core measure of balance-sheet profitability, indicating how efficiently a company converts its balance sheet into net interest income.
*   **Formula**: The calculation for Net Interest Margin is expressed as:
    $$\text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Interest Income} - \text{Interest Expense}}{\text{Average Earning Assets}}$$
*   **Operational Behavior**: NIM typically compresses when the cost of funding increases at a faster rate than the yields generated by assets.
