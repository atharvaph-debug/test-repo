# Travel & Expense Policy Overview

The Travel & Expense Policy is the corporate policy framework owned by Finance Shared Services that governs employee travel bookings, booking channels, flight classes, and reimbursements.

## Metadata Role

This policy is explicitly identified as part of the Travel & Expense Policy Governance Metadata.
