# Net Interest Margin Metric Overview

The Net Interest Margin (NIM), also known as nim, is a core profitability metric that measures the net interest income generated per dollar of interest-earning assets. It represents how efficiently a financial institution converts its balance sheet into net interest income and is reported in earnings and treasury reviews.

## Definition and Calculation

NIM is a key measure of balance-sheet profitability. It expresses the margin produced per dollar of earning assets and fluctuates with the interest-rate environment.

The Net Interest Margin is calculated using the following formula:

$$\text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Interest Income} - \text{Interest Expense}}{\text{Average Earning Assets}}$$

NIM must be actively monitored against the rate environment, as rising funding costs that outpace asset yields can compress the margin.
