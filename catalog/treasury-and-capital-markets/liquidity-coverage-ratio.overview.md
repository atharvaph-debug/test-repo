# Liquidity Coverage Ratio Overview

The Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) is a crucial Basel III short-term liquidity metric used in treasury and capital markets. It is calculated to assess an institution's ability to withstand a 30-calendar-day stress scenario.

## Key Features

The LCR is defined as High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) divided by total net cash outflows over a 30-calendar-day stress scenario. This metric has a strict regulatory binding floor of 100%. Any internal targets for LCR set above this 100% floor represent risk appetite buffers rather than statutory minimums.
