# Bill of Materials Overview

The Bill of Materials (BOM) is an authoritative, structured, and hierarchical "recipe" that links a finished-good Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) to its constituent component parts and sub-assemblies. Categorized under Inventory & Supply Chain, it serves as crucial metadata for product definition and manufacturing planning.

## Key Features

The Bill of Materials is characterized by its structured and hierarchical nature. It defines how a product is built, detailing all necessary components and sub-assemblies. Key aspects include:

*   **Hierarchical Structure**: BOMs are multi-level, allowing sub-assemblies to function as parent items with their own nested Bills of Materials. This enables a detailed breakdown of complex products.
*   **Component Linkage**: It explicitly links a finished-good SKU to all its required component parts and sub-assemblies. The Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) itself is the core internal identifier for a distinct, sellable product variant, encompassing specific attributes like size, color, and pack configuration.
*   **Planning System Integration**: The BOM is essential for planning systems, which "explode" parent SKUs through the BOM structure to accurately derive component demand for manufacturing and procurement.
