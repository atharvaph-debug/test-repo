# Supplier Scorecards Overview

Supplier Scorecards are a periodically refreshed rating system for active suppliers. They provide performance ratings based on key delivery, quality, and pricing metrics, primarily used to inform decisions for distributing purchasing volume.

## Key Performance Metrics

Supplier Scorecards combine three essential performance metrics:

*   **On-Time Delivery %:** This metric quantifies the share of purchase orders (POs) received by the promised date.
*   **Quality:** Supplier quality is assessed using defect, return, and rejection rates.
*   **Price Competitiveness:** This evaluates a supplier's pricing relative to alternative suppliers for the same part.

## Business Application

Buyers consult the Supplier Scorecards when a Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) requires replenishment. Suppliers with higher ratings are allocated a larger share of the order volume. New suppliers face a specific restriction: they are not permitted to receive production orders until an initial provisional scorecard has been established for them.
