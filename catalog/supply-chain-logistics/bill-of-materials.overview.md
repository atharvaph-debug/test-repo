# Bill of Materials Overview

A Bill of Materials (BOM), also known by its alias `bom`, is a hierarchical, engineering-controlled structured list that defines the exact components and sub-assemblies, along with their quantities, required to produce one unit of a finished-good Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) [Copy of Procurement SOP](1IXn1QX1m7lJ7kn6yGnQi8At_hhvBKsHuPGbs5SFUUI0), [Copy of Inventory Systems Overview](1Tp3jIXE-_Wfeb3gbFbouX0seOyi15etrylUYMzGWyh4), [Copy of BOM Spec](1DqruUuQIFjaVVYoBKmXnppaFmqCXj6PVZMom_hVOxxM). It serves as the authoritative recipe linking finished items to the procurement of raw components.

## Key Features

*   **Hierarchical Structure**: BOMs are multi-level and hierarchical, forming a tree structure. A sub-assembly defined within one BOM can itself have its own BOM. This design allows engineering to construct complex products from simpler parts and enables the reuse of sub-assemblies across multiple finished products [Copy of Inventory Systems Overview](1Tp3jIXE-_Wfeb3gbFbouX0seOyi15etrylUYMzGWyh4), [Copy of BOM Spec](1DqruUuQIFjaVVYoBKmXnppaFmqCXj6PVZMom_hVOxxM).
*   **Engineering Control**: The BOM is an engineering-controlled definition, ensuring accuracy and consistency in product assembly [Copy of Procurement SOP](1IXn1QX1m7lJ7kn6yGnQi8At_hhvBKsHuPGbs5SFUUI0), [Copy of Inventory Systems Overview](1Tp3jIXE-_Wfeb3gbFbouX0seOyi15etrylUYMzGWyh4), [Copy of BOM Spec](1DqruUuQIFjaVVYoBKmXnppaFmqCXj6PVZMom_hVOxxM).

## Operational Role

The Bill of Materials plays a critical role in procurement and planning operations:

*   **Demand Calculation**: Planning systems utilize the BOM hierarchy to "explode" finished-good SKU forecasts. This process automatically calculates component demand by multiplying the parent demand by the per-unit quantities at all levels of the BOM [Copy of Inventory Systems Overview](1Tp3jIXE-_Wfeb3gbFbouX0seOyi15etrylUYMzGWyh4), [Copy of BOM Spec](1DqruUuQIFjaVVYoBKmXnppaFmqCXj6PVZMom_hVOxxM).
*   **Single Source of Truth**: The BOM is considered the absolute single source of truth for component requirements. Buyers and planners are strictly prohibited from manually editing component quantities on a Purchase Order (PO) without first updating or verifying against the BOM [Copy of Procurement SOP](1IXn1QX1m7lJ7kn6yGnQi8At_hhvBKsHuPGbs5SFUUI0], [Copy of BOM Spec](1DqruUuQIFjaVVYoBKmXnppaFmqCXj6PVZMom_hVOxxM).
