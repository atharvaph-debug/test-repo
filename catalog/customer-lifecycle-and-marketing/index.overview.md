# Customer Lifecycle & Marketing

Analytics tracking customer value, marketing optimization, and loyalty performance.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/customer-lifecycle-and-marketing/customer-churn | Customer Churn | file | A subscriber-base metric representing the rate of customer departure or contract termination within a designated reporting timeframe. |
| catalog/customer-lifecycle-and-marketing/customer-lifetime-value | Customer Lifetime Value | file | A revenue-optimization metric that aggregates customer revenue and deducts expected cost-to-serve and retention expenses over an entire customer relationship. |
