# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), also known as LTV or Customer LTV, is a key revenue-optimization metric that aggregates customer revenue and deducts expected cost-to-serve and retention expenses over an entire customer relationship. It represents the total net profit expected from a customer over the duration of their relationship.

## Definition and Purpose

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) is defined as the total net profit a customer is expected to generate over their entire relationship. It serves as the primary metric for acquisition and spend optimization within growth marketing analytics, offering metadata definitions for these purposes.

## Calculation Methodology

The calculation of Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) involves subtracting the cost to serve and retain the customer from the projected revenue over their expected lifetime. This includes the cost-to-serve and retention spend subtracted from the total revenue, summed across the expected customer lifetime.
