# Customer Churn Overview

Customer churn, also known as customer attrition or churn rate, is a key subscriber-base metric that quantifies the rate at which customers depart or terminate their contracts within a designated reporting timeframe. It is a critical indicator for understanding customer loyalty and retention.

## Definition

Customer churn and customer attrition are equivalent metrics, representing the rate at which subscribers terminate service over a specified period. These terms are used interchangeably in analytical reporting to describe the loss of customers from a business.
