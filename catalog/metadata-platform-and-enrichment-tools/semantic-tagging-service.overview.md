# Semantic Tagging Service Overview

The Semantic Tagging Service (STS) is an automated system service designed for metadata enrichment, responsible for scanning unstructured and semi-structured assets to assign standardized category and entity tags. It analyzes metadata structures to apply taxonomical labels and business glossary terms, thereby enhancing the discoverability and understanding of data assets.

## Role in Metadata Enrichment

Within the broader "Metadata Enrichment" project, the Semantic Tagging Service plays a crucial role by automating the application of consistent metadata tagging. This directly supports the project's objectives of establishing coherent, cross-referenced data dictionaries and rule sets across various functional domains. By assigning standardized category and entity tags, the service helps to:
*   Ensure consistent metadata tagging to prevent analytical errors, such as double-counting, by aligning various terms to single metadata concepts.
*   Facilitate the application of defined domain glossaries and conceptual schemas, which span areas like Finance (e.g., Customer Lifetime Value, Credit Risk Score), Inventory & Supply Chain (e.g., SKU, Bill of Materials), and Telecommunications (e.g., IMSI, APN, QoS).
*   Enrich metadata fields to support their definition, storage, and utilization in various operations, including customer risk profiles, telecom network quality monitoring, and product supply chain management.
