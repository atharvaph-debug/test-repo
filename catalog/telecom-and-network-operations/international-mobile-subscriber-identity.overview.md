# International Mobile Subscriber Identity Overview

The International Mobile Subscriber Identity (IMSI) is a globally unique identifier that is provisioned directly onto a Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) card. It serves as the core authentication key during both local and roaming network attachments, representing the foundational layer for network authentication.

## Key Characteristics and Usage

The IMSI is a critical identifier within telecommunication systems, fulfilling several essential roles:

*   **Globally Unique Identifier**: It provides a unique identity for each subscriber, personalized onto the SIM card.
*   **Authentication Key**: The IMSI acts as the primary key for authenticating subscribers when they connect to a network, whether locally or while roaming.
*   **Data Management**: It is registered in subscriber databases, such as the Home Subscriber Server (HSS) and Home Location Register (HLR), and functions as the primary join key for mapping subscriber-level data.
*   **Roaming and Interconnect**: IMSI ranges are utilized in bilateral Roaming Partner Agreements. They help identify whether a subscriber is "home" or "visited," which in turn drives correct routing, enables service authorization (e.g., VoLTE support), and facilitates billing settlement processes.

In engineering documentation, the term **IMSI** is preferred over "SIM identity."
