# Securities Settlement Cycles Overview

Securities Settlement Cycles define the standardized operational timelines and cutoffs governing the transfer of securities and cash between transaction parties.

## Key Concepts

*   **T+1 Settlement**: This refers to the standard settlement cycle for equities and most fixed-income instruments. Under T+1 settlement, the settlement of a trade occurs exactly one business day after the trade date. For example, a trade executed on a Monday would settle on the following Tuesday.
*   **Calendar Extensions**: The settlement calendar is extended to account for holidays and weekends, meaning these days do not count towards the "one business day" in T+1 settlement.
