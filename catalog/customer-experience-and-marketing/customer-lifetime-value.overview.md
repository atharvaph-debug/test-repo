# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total net profit expected from a customer over their entire relationship with a business. This metric is crucial for understanding the long-term value a customer brings.

## Aliases and Definitions

This metric is also referred to as **customer LTV** or simply **LTV**. While `customer LTV` is explicitly defined as the total net profit expected from a customer over their entire relationship, the finance team's dashboards track this identical metric under the label **CLV**. Aligning these terms across departments is critical for cross-functional dashboard reconciliation.

## Calculation

Customer Lifetime Value is calculated as revenue minus cost-to-serve and retention spend.

## Related Concepts

Customer Churn & Attrition, which measures subscriber loss over a given period, directly impacts Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
