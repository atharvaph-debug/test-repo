# Customer Churn Overview

Customer Churn, also known as customer attrition or simply churn, measures the loss of subscribers over a specific period. It is reported as the share of deactivated subscribers during a given quarter and directly impacts Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

## Key Features

*   **Definition:** Customer Churn and Attrition are used interchangeably to describe the rate at which subscribers deactivate their services.
*   **Measurement:** It is reported as the share of deactivated subscribers during a specific quarter.
*   **Impact:** Customer churn directly affects the Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
