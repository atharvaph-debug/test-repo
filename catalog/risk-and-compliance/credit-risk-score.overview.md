# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score is a quantified estimate of a borrower's default likelihood. It is calculated by considering factors such as repayment history, current exposure, and income or leverage.

## Definition and Use
This score provides a quantified estimate of borrower default likelihood. It is utilized at the origination stage for making loan pricing and decision-making, as well as for ongoing monitoring of portfolio health.

## Data Distinction
It is crucial to maintain a strict distinction between the Credit Risk Score and the **KYC risk rating**. While the Credit Risk Score measures default risk, the KYC risk rating focuses on assessing financial-crime vulnerability.
