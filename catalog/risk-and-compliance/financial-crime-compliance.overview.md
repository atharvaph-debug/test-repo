# Financial Crime Compliance Overview

Financial Crime Compliance encompasses a suite of identity verification, continuous behavioral transaction monitoring, and regulatory filing workflows designed to mitigate financial crime. This domain includes core processes such as Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filings.

## Key Components

### Know Your Customer (KYC)
KYC is the foundational onboarding process focused on verifying customer identity documents and, for non-individuals, their beneficial ownership. It is also known as customer due diligence (CDD). The output of the KYC process is a risk rating, which can be "standard" or "enhanced." This rating subsequently dictates the level of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring required.

It is critical to distinguish the **KYC risk rating**, which measures financial-crime vulnerability, from the **Credit Risk Score**, which quantifies borrower default likelihood.

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
AML refers to ongoing, risk-based transaction monitoring activities. This process compares actual customer behaviors against expected patterns, leveraging the KYC risk rating to tailor the monitoring intensity.

### Suspicious Activity Report (SAR)
A SAR is a confidential, time-bound regulatory report filed when monitoring activities reveal suspected illicit financial activity. It is strictly prohibited to "tip off" customers about the filing of a SAR.
