# Financial and Regulatory Metrics

Metadata standards governing financial performance, balance sheet health, and regulatory compliance targets.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/financial-and-regulatory-metrics/customer-lifetime-value | Customer Lifetime Value | file | The total net profit expected from a customer over their entire relationship, driving marketing and acquisition spend limits. |
| catalog/financial-and-regulatory-metrics/liquidity-coverage-ratio | Liquidity Coverage Ratio | file | A primary short-term liquidity risk control metric measuring high-quality liquid assets against net cash outflows over a 30-day stress scenario. |
| catalog/financial-and-regulatory-metrics/net-interest-margin | Net Interest Margin | file | A key balance sheet profitability metric calculated as Net Interest Income divided by average interest-earning assets. |
