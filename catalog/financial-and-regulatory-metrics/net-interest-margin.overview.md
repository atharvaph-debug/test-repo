# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM), also known by its alias NIM, is a key balance sheet profitability metric used to assess the financial performance of an institution. It is calculated as Net Interest Income divided by average interest-earning assets.

## Definition and Formula

NIM is a crucial balance sheet profitability metric that measures the margin produced per dollar of earning assets. Its calculation involves:

$$\text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Net Interest Income}}{\text{Average Interest-Earning Assets}}$$

Net Interest Income itself is derived from the difference between the interest earned on assets and the interest paid on liabilities. The value of NIM can fluctuate based on the prevailing interest-rate environment.

## Reporting Scope

Net Interest Margin is formally reported in quarterly earnings and treasury reviews.
