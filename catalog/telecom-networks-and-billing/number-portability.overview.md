# Number Portability Overview

Number Portability refers to the administrative and technical process that allows a subscriber to retain their telephone number when changing service providers. This capability ensures that customers can transition between different telecommunications carriers without losing their established phone numbers.

## Definition and Terminology

Number portability describes the identical operational workflow where a customer keeps their existing phone number while transitioning between carriers. It represents a subscriber's ability to retain their telephone number when moving between a recipient (gaining) carrier and a donor (losing) carrier.

Common industry terms used interchangeably with Number Portability include:
*   **Porting**
*   **LNP** (Local Number Portability)
*   **MNP** (Mobile Number Portability)
