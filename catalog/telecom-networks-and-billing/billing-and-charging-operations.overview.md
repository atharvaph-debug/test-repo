# Billing and Charging Operations Overview

Billing and Charging Operations refer to the dual-stage processes of rating usage events according to price plans and aggregating those charges into periodic statements. This encompasses how customer usage is valued and subsequently presented as an invoice.

## Key Concepts

The overall process can be broken down into two distinct stages:

*   **Rating:** This stage involves applying a customer's specific price plan to individual usage events. The purpose of rating is to calculate a specific charge for each usage event.
*   **Billing:** Following rating, the billing stage aggregates these calculated charges. This aggregation occurs over a recurring billing cycle, ultimately leading to the generation of a customer invoice.
