# International Mobile Subscriber Identity Overview

The International Mobile Subscriber Identity (IMSI) is a globally unique cellular subscriber identifier. It is stored on the SIM card and registered in subscriber databases for network authentication and roaming authorization.

## Key Features

The IMSI plays a crucial role in cellular network operations:

*   **Authentication and Authorization**: When a device attaches to a visited network, the network reads the IMSI to differentiate the subscriber from local users. It identifies the subscriber's home operator and signals the home network to authenticate the subscriber and authorize service. Roaming relies on the IMSI for this differentiation and signaling process.
*   **SIM Provisioning**: Every SIM card is personalized with an IMSI during the provisioning process. This IMSI is then registered in the subscriber database (e.g., HSS/HLR), making it a fundamental component for subscriber identity management.
