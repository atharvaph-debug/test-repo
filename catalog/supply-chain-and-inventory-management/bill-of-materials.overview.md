# Bill of Materials Overview

A Bill of Materials (BOM) is the authoritative, hierarchical engineering specification detailing the components and sub-assemblies required to manufacture a product. It serves as the bridge between engineering design and procurement shopping lists.

## Key Features and Role

The BOM is a structured engineering definition that describes what goes into a product. It is hierarchical and multi-level, listing parent Stock Keeping Units (SKUs) down to lower-level sub-assemblies and raw components, which allows for reuse across various products.

It acts as the single source of truth, detailing every component and the quantity required to manufacture one finished unit. The BOM is critical for procurement operations, as automated planning systems utilize it to explode purchase requisitions. Buyers are instructed never to manually edit Purchase Order (PO) quantities without first cross-checking the BOM.
