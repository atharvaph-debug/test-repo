# Portability and Attrition Overview

"Portability and Attrition" refers to the operational tracking of carrier-to-carrier telephone number migration and the customer churn rates captured during retention workflows. This concept is central to metadata related to subscriber usage transformation, charging cycles, porting workflows, and overall business attrition.

## Key Concepts

*   **Number Portability**: This occurs when a customer moves their telephone number from one carrier to another. Operational terms synonymous with number portability include "porting", "LNP" (Local Number Portability), and "MNP" (Mobile Number Portability).
*   **Churn / Attrition**: This measures the rate at which subscribers discontinue their service and leave the carrier. During retention workflows, care agents capture specific churn metadata, which can include factors such as pricing concerns, coverage issues, or competitive offers from other providers.

## Aliases

This entry is also known by "churn", "number portability", "porting", "LNP" (Local Number Portability), and "MNP" (Mobile Number Portability).
