# Know Your Customer Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC), also known as Customer Due Diligence (CDD), is a crucial process within risk, compliance, and security frameworks. It involves verifying a customer's identity, establishing beneficial ownership for non-individual entities, and assigning a risk rating.

## Key Features

*   **Core Process**: KYC encompasses the verification of a customer's identity and, for non-individual entities, the establishment of beneficial ownership.
*   **Risk Rating**: A key output of the KYC process is the assignment of a risk rating, which can be either "Standard" or "Enhanced." This rating is determined at the time of customer onboarding and is periodically refreshed.
*   **Operational Context**: The customer risk rating derived from KYC serves as a foundational parameter for subsequent downstream Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring activities.
*   **Distinction from Credit Risk**: It is important to note that KYC risk ratings are distinct from Credit Risk Scores, which quantify a borrower’s default probability.
*   **Relationship with AML**: While KYC focuses on verifying *who* the customer is, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitors *what* they do, identifying patterns inconsistent with expected behavior.
*   **Metadata Dependencies**: The KYC Risk Rating directly influences AML Monitoring, which in turn can lead to the generation of Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR) as a final output, forming a critical metadata dependency chain: KYC Risk Rating $\rightarrow$ AML Monitoring $\rightarrow$ SAR.
