# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score is a quantified estimate of a borrower's default probability. This score is evaluated using factors such as repayment history, current exposure, and the borrower's leverage or income.

## Operational Scope

Credit Risk Scores are primarily used during the origination of new loans and for ongoing portfolio monitoring. They play a crucial role in driving lending decisions, with lower scores indicating a higher default risk for the borrower.

## Important Distinctions

It is essential to distinguish Credit Risk Scores from Know Your Customer (KYC) risk ratings, as they serve different purposes and are based on different criteria.
