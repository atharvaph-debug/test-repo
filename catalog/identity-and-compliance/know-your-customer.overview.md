# Know Your Customer Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC), also known as `kyc`, `kyc-protocols`, and `compliance-controls`, refers to the regulatory identification and risk validation protocols performed during initial onboarding and periodically throughout the customer relationship. It is a foundational process for verifying a customer's identity and assessing the risk they pose to an institution.

## Core Objectives
The core objective of KYC is to verify a customer's identity and assess the risk they pose to the institution. This process is initiated during onboarding and refreshed periodically thereafter.

## Key Requirements and Process
No new bank account is activated until KYC processes are complete.
*   **Individual Customers**: KYC requires verifying identity documents.
*   **Non-Individual Customers**: KYC requires establishing beneficial ownership.

Onboarding agents are responsible for logging the verification results and the customer's KYC risk rating, as described in the [Customer Onboarding SOP](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8847361). These conditions must be satisfied before an account is activated, as per the [Compliance Policy Handbook](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8454166).

## Relationship with Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
KYC reviews are a critical component in anti-money laundering efforts. When transaction monitoring and KYC reviews establish a reasonable suspicion of financial crime, investigators must escalate cases and file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR). A strict "No tipping off" rule applies, meaning under no circumstances may the customer be informed that a SAR is being contemplated or has been filed, according to the [AML Investigations Playbook](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8683541).
