# AML Investigations & Escalation Overview

This entry describes the workflow for evaluating transaction anomalies, generating suspicious activity files, and maintaining regulatory communication confidentiality within the context of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) processes.

## Key Features

*   **Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR):** Investigators are required to escalate cases and file a SAR when transaction monitoring and Know Your Customer (KYC) reviews establish a reasonable suspicion of financial crime.
*   **"No tipping off" Rule:** A strict "No tipping off" rule applies, meaning that under no circumstances may the customer be informed that a SAR is being contemplated or has been filed.
