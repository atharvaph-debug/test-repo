# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score is a quantified estimate of a borrower's likelihood of defaulting on financial obligations.

## Definition and Purpose

This score represents a quantified estimate of a borrower’s default likelihood. It is built from factors such as repayment history, current exposure, and income/leverage. The Credit Risk Score is used to drive pricing decisions, including setting credit limits and interest rates, and feeds into the aggregation of portfolio expected-loss provisioning.

## Metadata Distinction

A critical metadata distinction must be maintained between a customer's **KYC risk rating**, which measures financial-crime risk, and their **Credit Risk Score**, which measures the likelihood of default. These two scores cannot be conflated.
