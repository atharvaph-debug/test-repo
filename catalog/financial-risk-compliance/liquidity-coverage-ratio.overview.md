# Liquidity Coverage Ratio Overview

The Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) is a Basel III regulatory metric measuring the proportion of high-quality liquid assets held by a financial institution to meet short-term cash outflows. It is designed to monitor a financial institution's short-term resilience under stress [Copy of Basel III Filing Summary](1REJ7VjzTcvcEAiviGYU0psJZhCjTjxDPJ6NIHf5zZqQ), [Copy of Risk Management Framework](1qWcFBL9LnXxm2iOegMjnO4_Qywbt17Kw-hAOH-arSUs).

## Definition and Calculation

The LCR is defined as the ratio of high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) to projected net cash outflows over a 30-calendar-day stress scenario [Copy of Basel III Filing Summary](1REJ7VjzTcvcEAiviGYU0psJZhCjTjxDPJ6NIHf5zZqQ), [Copy of Risk Management Framework](1qWcFBL9LnXxm2iOegMjnO4_Qywbt17Kw-hAOH-arSUs). This calculation is governed under Basel III regulatory standards [Copy of Treasury Operations Guide](1kXDYTun6V0lc4d83F_HS1g-L6G_iTsTFt6eoYnTGtwA).

The formula for LCR is:

```
LCR = High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) / 30-day Net Cash Outflows
```

## Regulatory and Operational Targets

Basel III rules establish a binding regulatory floor for the LCR, strictly set at **100%**. An intraday breach of this floor is considered a notable regulatory event that requires immediate escalation [Copy of Basel III Filing Summary](1REJ7VjzTcvcEAiviGYU0psJZhCjTjxDPJ6NIHf5zZqQ).

In addition to the regulatory floor, the organization maintains an internal operating target for the LCR. This internal target is approximately **110%**, providing a buffer for normal fluctuations. The LCR is monitored daily against this internal management target [Copy of Risk Management Framework](1qWcFBL9LnXxm2iOegMjnO4_Qywbt17Kw-hAOH-arSUs), [Copy of Treasury Operations Guide](1kXDYTun6V0lc4d83F_HS1g-L6G_iTsTFt6eoYnTGtwA).

## Monitoring

Monitoring of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio is owned by Group Treasury and the Asset-Liability Management (ALM) team [Copy of Treasury Operations Guide](1kXDYTun6V0lc4d83F_HS1g-L6G_iTsTFt6eoYnTGtwA).
