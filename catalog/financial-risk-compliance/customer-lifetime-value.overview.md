# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total net profit expected from a customer over their entire relationship, aligning both marketing and financial metrics.

## Aliases

This metric is also known as Customer LTV. The terms Customer LTV (used by Growth Marketing Analytics) and CLV (Customer Lifetime Value, used by the Finance team and in the Finance Business Glossary) refer to the exact same metric and should not be treated as distinct measures.

## Definition and Calculation

Customer Lifetime Value is defined as the total net profit expected from a customer over their entire relationship. It is calculated as projected revenue minus cost-to-serve and retention spend.

## Application

CLV is used to determine sustainable acquisition spend. High-LTV/CLV segments, such as the premium-account channel, warrant higher acquisition and retention investment.
