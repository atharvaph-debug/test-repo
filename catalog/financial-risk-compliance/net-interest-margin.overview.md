# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM) is a core balance-sheet profitability metric managed by Group Treasury. It serves as a key operational and balance sheet metric required for internal treasury oversight and regulatory filings, measuring net interest income relative to average interest-earning assets.

## Key Features

NIM is calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets.

The calculation formula is:
$\text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Interest Income} - \text{Interest Expense}}{\text{Average Earning Assets}}$

### Behavioral Metadata
*   NIM compresses when funding costs rise faster than asset yields.
*   NIM expands when asset yields outpace funding costs.
