# Know Your Customer Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC), also known by its alias `kyc`, refers to the onboarding and continuous verification process used to establish customer identity, beneficial ownership, and financial-crime risk profiles within the Financial Risk & Compliance category. It is a critical component in distinguishing regulatory compliance markers and framing downstream risk decisions.

## Role as Metadata

A customer's **KYC risk rating** is a crucial metadata distinction, measuring financial-crime risk. It must be carefully differentiated from their **credit risk score**, which measures the likelihood of default; these two metrics cannot be conflated ([Copy of Customer Onboarding SOP](1Axp8e_zvhvHjyJ0B3sSuDRzINv-Zt8r9aJVpFGILAtE), [Copy of Finance Business Glossary](1lX_2XEm4jpiN-gNOv30UEBoJ1eYAvDUl2BL-g-FZ2KE)). The KYC risk rating is established at onboarding and forms the metadata baseline for ongoing downstream monitoring efforts ([Copy of Compliance Policy Handbook](10ivoRcx7RyKcugHb7zVnJSgIRV98XZgVjtV10CZro04), [Copy of Customer Onboarding SOP](1Axp8e_zvhvHjyJ0B3sSuDRzINv-Zt8r9aJVpFGILAtE)).

## Compliance Lifecycle

KYC is the initial step in the compliance lifecycle pipeline. It is established during customer onboarding through verifying identity and beneficial ownership, employing either standard or enhanced due diligence ([Copy of Compliance Policy Handbook](10ivoRcx7RyKcugHb7zVnJSgIRV98XZgVjtV10CZro04), [Copy of Customer Onboarding SOP](1Axp8e_zvhvHjyJ0B3sSuDRzINv-Zt8r9aJVpFGILAtE)). The KYC risk profile then directly informs subsequent stages, such as:
1.  **AML (Anti-Money Laundering) Monitoring:** Ongoing, risk-based transaction monitoring that uses the KYC risk profile to detect deviations like unexpected transaction volumes or structuring patterns ([Copy of Compliance Policy Handbook](10ivoRcx7RyKcugHb7zVnJSgIRV98XZgVjtV10CZro04), [Copy of AML Investigations Playbook](1dSWNVjfYvUmeb-kA8ETaZ38K6YCJG7SkC5oOksvxKeo)).
2.  **SAR (Suspicious Activity Report):** The formal output when an investigation confirms reasonable suspicion, subject to strict regulatory timelines and "no tipping off" policies ([Copy of Compliance Policy Handbook](10ivoRcx7RyKcugHb7zVnJSgIRV98XZgVjtV10CZro04), [Copy of AML Investigations Playbook](1dSWNVjfYvUmeb-kA8ETaZ38K6YCJG7SkC5oOksvxKeo)).
