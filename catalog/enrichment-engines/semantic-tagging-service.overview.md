# Semantic Tagging Service Overview

The Semantic Tagging Service (STS), also known as "semantic-tagger," is an automated service designed for metadata enrichment. It analyzes metadata structures to apply taxonomical labels and business glossary terms, thereby enhancing the discoverability and understanding of data assets.
