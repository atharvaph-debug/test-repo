# Finance & Customer Analytics

Standardized metrics and terms tracking financial performance and customer lifetime value.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/finance-and-customer-analytics/customer-churn | Customer Churn | file | The rate at which subscribers deactivate or leave service over a specific period, calculated as a percentage of the total subscriber base. |
| catalog/finance-and-customer-analytics/customer-lifetime-value | Customer Lifetime Value | file | The total expected net profit earned from a customer over the entire duration of their relationship. |
| catalog/finance-and-customer-analytics/net-interest-margin | Net Interest Margin | file | A profitability metric calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets. |
