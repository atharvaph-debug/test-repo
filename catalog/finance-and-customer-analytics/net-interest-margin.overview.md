# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM) is a profitability metric calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets. It measures the margin a balance sheet produces per dollar of earning assets.

## Definition and Calculation

Net Interest Margin is defined as net interest income, which is the difference between interest earned on assets and interest paid on liabilities, divided by average interest-earning assets. This metric provides insight into the profitability of an organization's interest-generating activities.

## Operational Context

NIM is a key financial indicator that is reported during earnings and treasury reviews. Its value fluctuates based on the prevailing interest-rate environment, reflecting its sensitivity to market conditions.

## Aliases

This metric is also known by its alias, NIM.
