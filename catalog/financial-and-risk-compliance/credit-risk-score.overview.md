# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score, also known as Credit Risk Rating, is a mathematical scoring that quantifies the financial probability of a borrower's default on a loan or product. This score is distinct from anti-money laundering risk scores, such as the KYC Risk Rating, which measures financial-crime vulnerability rather than default probability.

## Key Features

*   **Purpose**: The Credit Risk Score is designed to quantify the financial probability of loan or product default for individual borrowers.
*   **Calculation**: It is calculated using several factors, including historical repayment patterns, existing credit exposures, and customer leverage ratios or income factors.
*   **Usage**: This score plays a critical role in various financial operations, such as:
    *   Lending decisions
    *   Loan pricing
    *   Monitoring portfolio health
    *   Influencing underwriting pricing
    *   Aggregated expected-loss provisioning
*   **Distinction**: It is important to distinguish the Credit Risk Score from the KYC Risk Rating. While Credit Risk Score assesses the likelihood of financial default, the KYC Risk Rating quantifies financial-crime and money-laundering risk (e.g., *Standard* vs. *Enhanced* due diligence levels). Data platforms must ensure high data quality by correctly distinguishing these two key ratings on the Customer Profile.
