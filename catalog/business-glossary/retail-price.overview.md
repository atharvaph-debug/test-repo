# Retail Price Overview

Retail Price represents the catalog or list price for a product as advertised to customers. This is the price before any discounts or promotions have been applied.

## Key Features

The Retail Price is the publicly displayed reference price for products. It is distinct from the **Cost**, which is the wholesale or landed price paid to the supplier, and the **Sale Price**, which is the actual amount paid by the customer after all discounts and promotions, used for realized revenue calculations.

## Metadata

*   **Canonical Name**: Retail Price
*   **Aliases**: `products.retail_price`
*   **Definition**: The reference catalog or list price advertised to customers.
*   **Location**: This price can be found in the `products.retail_price` column.
*   **Application**: It represents the price *pre-discount*, meaning before any reductions or promotions are applied to the customer.
