# Cost Overview

Cost refers to the wholesale or landed price paid to the supplier for a product. This value is considered an internal metric and is hidden from the customer-facing catalog.

## Key Features

The Cost represents the initial expense incurred by the business for acquiring goods. It is distinct from customer-facing prices such as the Retail Price (the advertised list price) and the Sale Price (the actual transaction amount paid by the customer).

## Metadata

The Cost attribute can be found in the following locations within the data:
*   `products.cost`
*   `inventory_items.cost`
