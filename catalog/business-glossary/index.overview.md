# Business Glossary

Definitions of core catalog metrics, price attributes, and sales transactions in the eCommerce business model.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/business-glossary/cost | Cost | file | The wholesale or landed price paid to the supplier, hidden from the customer facing catalog. |
| catalog/business-glossary/retail-price | Retail Price | file | The catalog or list price advertised to customers before any discounts or promotions are applied. |
| catalog/business-glossary/sale-price | Sale Price | file | The actual realized transaction amount paid by the customer, utilized as the source of truth for revenue calculations. |
