# Sale Price Overview

Sale Price represents the actual amount paid by a customer in a transaction. It serves as the definitive source for all realized revenue calculations.

## Key Features

The **Sale Price** is the finalized transaction amount that a customer pays. This value is distinct from the `Retail Price`, which is the advertised list price before any discounts, and `Cost`, which is the wholesale or landed price paid to the supplier. The Sale Price is considered the authoritative column for computing all realized revenue.

## Source

The Sale Price is documented in the `order_items.sale_price` column.
