# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM) is a key performance metric that measures balance-sheet profitability. It is also known by its acronym, NIM.

## Definition and Calculation

NIM is a standard profitability metric used by financial professionals. It is calculated by dividing net interest income by average interest-earning assets. Net interest income is defined as the interest earned on assets minus the interest payments made on liabilities. It measures the difference between interest earned on assets and interest paid on liabilities, expressed relative to total interest-earning assets.

The formula is:
```
Net Interest Margin = (Net Interest Income) / (Average Interest-Earning Assets)
```
Where:
```
Net Interest Income = (Interest Earned on Assets) - (Interest Payments Made on Liabilities)
```

## Purpose

Net Interest Margin serves as a core metric to assess balance-sheet profitability. It is utilized by analysts, product, and reporting teams to evaluate the efficiency and profitability of a financial institution's interest-generating activities.
