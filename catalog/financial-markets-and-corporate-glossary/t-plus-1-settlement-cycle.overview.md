# T+1 Settlement Cycle Overview

The T+1 Settlement Cycle, also known as the T+1 standard, is a standard transactional cycle in financial markets where the clearing and settlement of trades must occur exactly one business day after the initial trade date. This cycle applies to equities and most fixed-income instruments.

## Key Features

The T+1 Settlement Cycle operates on a standard cycle, requiring the clearing and settlement of trades for equities and most fixed-income instruments to be finalized one business day following the original trade date.
