# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) is an analytic term that measures the projected total net profit a customer is expected to generate over the entire duration of their relationship with a business. This metric is crucial for understanding the long-term value of customers and informing strategic decisions.

## Aliases and Cross-Mapping

While fundamentally the same metric, Customer Lifetime Value is known by different aliases depending on the team or context:
*   **LTV**: Commonly used by growth marketing teams.
*   **CLV**: Frequently used by finance teams.

To ensure analytical consistency and facilitate metadata enrichment across different data models and departmental perspectives, these terms must be mapped as synonymous. This cross-mapping is essential for unified reporting and analysis, allowing various teams to refer to the same underlying concept despite using different nomenclature.
