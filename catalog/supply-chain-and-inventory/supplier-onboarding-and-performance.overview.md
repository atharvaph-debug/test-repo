# Supplier Onboarding & Performance Overview

Supplier Onboarding & Performance is the administrative framework used to register suppliers and assess their ongoing capability across key metrics such as delivery, quality, and pricing. It involves the establishment and periodic evaluation of supplier scorecards, also known as vendor ratings, for every active supplier.

## Aliases

This entry is also known as `vendor-rating` and `supplier-scorecards`.

## Key Metrics

Supplier scorecards, which are central to evaluating supplier performance, combine three core metrics:

*   **On-time delivery %**: This metric measures the share of Purchase Orders (POs) that are received by their promised date.
*   **Quality**: Supplier quality is evaluated through an analysis of defect, return, and rejection rates.
*   **Price competitiveness**: This assesses a supplier's pricing relative to alternative suppliers who offer the same part or service.
