# Supplier Performance Scorecarding Overview

The Supplier Performance Scorecarding, also known as vendor rating, is an evaluation system designed to track and assess the performance of active suppliers. This system periodically updates a scorecard based on key operational criteria to ensure continuous evaluation.

## Key Features and Performance Metrics

The scorecard aggregates three main operational criteria to provide a comprehensive view of supplier performance:

*   **On-time delivery %:** This metric is calculated as the ratio of purchase orders (POs) that were successfully received by the promised delivery date.
*   **Quality:** Supplier quality is assessed based on monitored defect, return, and rejection rates.
*   **Price Competitiveness:** This criterion involves a relative comparison of the supplier's pricing against alternate suppliers for identical parts.
