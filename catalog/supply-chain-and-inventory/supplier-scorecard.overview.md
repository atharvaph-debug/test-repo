# Supplier Scorecard Overview

The Supplier Scorecard, also known as Vendor Rating, is a structured, periodically refreshed rating system designed to assess supplier performance. It aggregates key performance indicators related to on-time delivery, quality, and price competitiveness. Buyers utilize this scorecard to allocate order volume, especially when multiple qualified sources are available for a component.

## Key Performance Indicators

The scorecard aggregates three primary performance indicators:

*   **On-time delivery %**: This measures the share of purchase orders (POs) received by the promised delivery date.
*   **Quality**: This metric is derived from defect, return, and rejection rates associated with a supplier's products.
*   **Price competitiveness**: This evaluates a supplier's pricing relative to alternate suppliers for the same part.

## Usage and Maturity

The Supplier Scorecard plays a crucial role in procurement by guiding buyers in the allocation of order volume among qualified suppliers. It is applied after the initial supplier qualification phase, during which no production orders can be placed. New suppliers initially receive a provisional score based on trial orders. This provisional rating evolves into a standard rating as real purchase order history accumulates over time.
