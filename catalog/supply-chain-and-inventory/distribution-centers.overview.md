# Distribution Centers Overview

Distribution Centers (DCs) are key inventory consolidation and fulfillment nodes within a supply chain, classified hierarchically by their regional and forward operational proximity. They serve as crucial logistics nodes positioned between upstream suppliers and downstream demand.

## Key Functions and Role

Distribution Centers play a vital role in logistics by:
*   **Receiving Inventory:** Taking in goods in bulk quantities.
*   **Warehousing Storage:** Managing the storage of inventory.
*   **Order Fulfillment:** Fulfilling downstream orders for both retail stores and end customers.

They are facilities designed to store inventory and fulfill downstream demand, forming a critical part of the overall network strategy.

## Classification and Tiers

The metadata framework for Distribution Centers tracks a tiered network, classifying them based on their strategic location and function:
*   **Regional DCs:** These centers hold a broad assortment of inventory and are responsible for replenishing downstream nodes within a larger geographical area.
*   **Forward DCs:** Located physically closer to demand centers, these DCs enable faster fulfillment to specific customer or store locations.
