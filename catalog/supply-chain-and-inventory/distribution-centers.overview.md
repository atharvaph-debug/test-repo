# Distribution Centers Overview

Distribution Centers (DCs) are physical fulfillment nodes within a logistics network, designed to route inventory from upstream suppliers to downstream demand centers. They serve as inventory storage and fulfillment hubs, forming a critical component of the supply chain infrastructure.

## Key Features

*   **Role in Logistics**: DCs are integral to the `DC Topology`, which describes the physical logistics network that routes inventory. They function as central points for both storing and fulfilling inventory needs.
*   **Tiered Structure**: The operations model for distribution centers establishes a tiered layout. This includes two explicit tiers of fulfillment nodes:
    *   **Regional Distribution Centers (Regional DCs)**
    *   **Forward Distribution Centers (Forward DCs)**
