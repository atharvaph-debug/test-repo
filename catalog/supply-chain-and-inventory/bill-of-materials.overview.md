# Bill of Materials Overview

A Bill of Materials (BOM) is a precise, engineering-defined hierarchy that lists the components, sub-assemblies, and quantities required to produce a final finished-good Stock Keeping Unit (SKU). It serves as the authoritative, structured engineering definition of what goes into a product.

## Key Characteristics & Contents

The BOM is structured metadata consisting of a comprehensive list that documents:
*   Every **component**
*   Every **sub-assembly**
*   The exact **quantity** needed to construct one unit of a finished-good SKU.

## Role in Supply Chain & Procurement

The Bill of Materials acts as a critical bridge connecting engineering designs with procurement activity. Within procurement and manufacturing workflows, this structured data enables "requisition explosion" to ensure accurate material replenishment. For any given finished-good Stock Keeping Unit (SKU), the BOM explicitly details its constituent parts.
