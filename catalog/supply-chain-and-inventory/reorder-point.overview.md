# Reorder Point Overview

The Reorder Point (ROP), also known by its alias ROP, is a critical supply chain and inventory management metric. It is defined as a dynamic target formula calculated as (Average Daily Demand \* Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock.

## Key Features

The Reorder Point is calculated using the following formula:
$$\text{ROP} = (\text{Average Daily Demand} \times \text{Lead Time in Days}) + \text{Safety Stock}$$

This metric is dynamically calculated by the planning system. Any changes made to lead time or safety stock parameters will automatically update the active Reorder Point, ensuring its responsiveness to evolving inventory conditions.
