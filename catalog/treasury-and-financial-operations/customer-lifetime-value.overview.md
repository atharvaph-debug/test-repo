# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), also known as Customer LTV or LTV, represents the projected net profit expected to be generated over the entire duration of a customer relationship. It is a key metric in Treasury & Financial Operations.

## Calculation

The Customer Lifetime Value is calculated as the projected net profit over the lifetime of a customer relationship, using the formula:
$\text{Revenue} - \text{Cost-to-Serve} - \text{Retention Spend}$

## Taxonomy and Aliases

The terms "Customer LTV," "LTV," and "CLV" all refer to the exact same metric. While marketing decks typically use "LTV" and finance dashboards commonly use "CLV," downstream analytical systems are designed to resolve these differing terms to a single unified metadata attribute for consistency and accurate reporting.
