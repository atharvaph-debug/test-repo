# Treasury & Financial Operations

Analytical formulas, cash flow ratios, and trading/settlement cutoffs used in finance and treasury.

| Path | Title | Type | Description |
|------|-------|------|-------------|
| catalog/treasury-and-financial-operations/customer-lifetime-value | Customer Lifetime Value | file | The projected net profit generated over the entire duration of a customer relationship. |
| catalog/treasury-and-financial-operations/liquidity-coverage-ratio | Liquidity Coverage Ratio | file | Basel III regulatory ratio defined as high-quality liquid assets divided by total 30-day net cash outflows under stress. |
| catalog/treasury-and-financial-operations/net-interest-margin | Net Interest Margin | file | A profitability metric calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets. |
| catalog/treasury-and-financial-operations/trade-settlement | Trade Settlement | file | The process finalizing a trade where cash and securities are exchanged to discharge transaction obligations, heavily reliant on daily cutoffs. |
