# Voice over LTE Overview

Voice over LTE (VoLTE), also known as IMS Voice, is a technology that routes voice calls as packetized data over LTE networks. This process utilizes the IP Multimedia Subsystem (IMS) core and employs high-priority Quality of Service (QoS) controls to ensure call clarity.

## Technology and Routing

VoLTE, synonymous with "Voice-over-LTE" and "IMS voice", routes voice calls as packetized data across the LTE network. Unlike traditional methods that use legacy circuit-switched voice paths, VoLTE leverages the IP Multimedia Subsystem (IMS) core for this routing [[Network Engineering Glossary](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8617985)].

## Quality of Service (QoS)

VoLTE calls are dependent on a dedicated, high-priority QoS class to guarantee call clarity. The target thresholds for this QoS are formalized within the system's QoS Policy [[Network Engineering Glossary](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8617985)]. High-priority voice signaling for VoLTE is carried across a distinct IMS Access Point Name (APN) [[Telecom Systems & Terminology Overview](https://atharva-test-izfs7ihz.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KCD/pages/8552489)].
