# Office Access and Badging Overview

Office Access and Badging policies coordinate visitor clearance guidelines and badging controls, distinct from general warehouse parameters. These policies define rules for who can access office facilities and how physical badges are managed.

## Scope and Applicability

Office Access and Badging Policies govern specific administrative aspects, including:
*   Visitor access
*   Day passes
*   Physical badging

These policies are exclusively tagged as `Corporate Office / Workplace Services` [Copy of Office Badge Facilities Policy.docx](https://atharvasptest.sharepoint.com/sites/agent-demo/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B7E679034-E2E3-48DD-81E3-13572ED27B20%7D&file=Copy%20of%20Office%20Badge%20Facilities%20Policy.docx&action=default&mobileredirect=true).

## Exclusion Rules

An explicit exclusion rule states that these policies must be skipped for database operations tagged under `Warehouse Ops` or `Distribution Centers` [Copy of Office Badge Facilities Policy.docx](https://atharvasptest.sharepoint.com/sites/agent-demo/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B7E679034-E2E3-48DD-81E3-13572ED27B20%7D&file=Copy%20of%20Office%20Badge%20Facilities%20Policy.docx&action=default&mobileredirect=true). This ensures that policies intended for office environments are not incorrectly applied to operational warehouse or distribution center contexts.
