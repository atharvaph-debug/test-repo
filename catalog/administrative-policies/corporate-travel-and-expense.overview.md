# Corporate Travel and Expense Overview

Corporate Travel and Expense monitors business travel constraints, class limitations, and non-operational expense reporting compliance. Classified under Administrative Policies, this entry refers to guidelines that govern corporate expenditures which are not related to operational activities.

## Policy Details
The policy for Corporate Travel and Expense outlines several key requirements and restrictions:
*   **Booking Method**: All corporate travel bookings must be made using a designated corporate travel tool.
*   **Flight Class**: For flights with a duration under 6 hours, employees are required to book economy class.
*   **Submission Deadline**: Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the spend occurring.
*   **Personal Expenses**: Strict isolation of personal expenses from corporate expense reports is mandated.

## Governance and Scope
This policy is governed by Finance Shared Services. It explicitly holds no relationship to customer, risk, or treasury operations, focusing purely on non-operational corporate expenditures.
