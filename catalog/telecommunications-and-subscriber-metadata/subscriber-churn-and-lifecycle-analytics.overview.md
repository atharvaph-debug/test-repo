# Subscriber Churn and Lifecycle Analytics Overview

This entry provides metadata for understanding and managing subscriber attrition, contract status, and customer lifetime value within the telecommunications sector. It connects customer care operations, commercial lifecycle statuses, billing system logic, and subscriber churn risk profiles.

## Key Concepts and Definitions

*   **Customer Churn / Attrition**: These terms are analytically equivalent and refer to the percentage of the subscriber base that deactivates during a specific quarter.
    *   Voluntary churn is primarily driven by factors such as Price, Network Quality, and Competitor Offers.
    *   Churn analysis is detailed in the Churn Analysis — Quarterly Report and related to the Customer Care Handbook.

*   **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)**: This metric represents the total net profit expected from a customer throughout their entire relationship with the company. It is calculated as projected revenue minus the combined cost to serve and retain them.
    *   Finance dashboards typically label this metric as **CLV**, while marketing decks use **customer LTV** or simply **LTV**. These terms denote the same metric and should not be treated as distinct measures, as noted in the Finance Business Glossary and Marketing Analytics — Deck Notes.

## Subscriber Metadata for Churn Risk and Retention

This topic emphasizes how specific subscriber attributes serve as critical metadata for assessing churn risk and informing retention strategies:

*   **Contract Status**: Subscribers who are out-of-contract are significantly more prone to price-driven churn. This status is a key metadata point for identifying vulnerable segments.
*   **Product Depth**: Single-product subscribers exhibit higher churn susceptibility compared to multi-service subscribers, indicating that deeper product relationships result in greater customer stickiness. The number of products a subscriber uses is therefore important enrichment metadata.
*   **Targeting Logic**: To effectively direct retention and incentive budgets, subscriber profiles are mapped against their Customer Lifetime Value (CLV). This allows for targeting high-value, at-risk subscriber segments.

These metadata attributes and their relationship to churn are discussed in the Churn Analysis — Quarterly Report.

## Related Financial Metrics

The Finance Business Glossary also references other financial metrics, distinct from subscriber churn and CLV but relevant for broader financial analysis:

*   **Net Interest Margin (NIM)**: A profitability metric reported in earnings and treasury reviews, calculated as:
    $$\text{Net Interest Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Interest Income}}{\text{Average Interest-Earning Assets}}$$
    NIM expresses the margin produced per dollar of earning assets and fluctuates with the interest-rate environment.
*   **Credit Risk Score**: A quantified estimate of a borrower's probability of default, derived from repayment history, current exposure, and leverage/income. It is used for loan pricing and portfolio monitoring.
    *   This score must be distinguished from the **KYC Risk Rating**, which specifically assesses financial-crime risk rather than credit default risk.
