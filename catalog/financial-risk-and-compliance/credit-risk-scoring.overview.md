# Credit Risk Scoring Overview

Credit Risk Scoring is a predictive estimate of a borrower's likelihood of default, derived from repayment history, financial exposure, and leverage ratios. It is quantified at the individual borrower level and represents the likelihood that a borrower will fail to meet their repayment obligations.

## Definition

A Credit Risk Score is a predictive estimate of default, quantifying the risk that an individual borrower will fail to meet their repayment obligations.

## Data Inputs

The Credit Risk Score is derived from several key data inputs:
*   Borrower repayment history
*   Current financial exposure
*   Income-to-leverage ratios

## Lifecycle Applications

Credit Risk Scores are utilized across various stages of the financial lifecycle:
*   **Origination**: Used to inform lending decisions and determine appropriate loan pricing.
*   **Portfolio Monitoring**: Continuously applied to track the overall health and performance of a portfolio.
*   **Loss Provisioning**: Aggregated across the entire book, these scores directly feed into models that calculate expected loss provisions.
