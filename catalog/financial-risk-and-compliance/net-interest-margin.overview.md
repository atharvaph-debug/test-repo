# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM) is a fundamental profitability metric used to assess the efficiency and performance of financial institutions. It is a key indicator of balance sheet profitability.

## Aliases

NIM

## Definition and Calculation

Net Interest Margin is calculated as net interest income divided by average interest-earning assets. More specifically, the core calculation is:

`NIM = (interest income − interest expense) / average earning assets`

It represents the efficiency with which a financial institution converts its balance sheet into net interest income.

## Significance

NIM is considered a core measure of balance-sheet profitability, indicating how effectively a financial institution is managing its interest-earning assets and interest-bearing liabilities. A compression in NIM can occur when funding costs (interest expense) rise faster than the yields generated from interest-earning assets (interest income).
