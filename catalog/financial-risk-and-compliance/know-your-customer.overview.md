# Know Your Customer Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC), also known interchangeably as Customer Due Diligence (CDD), is a mandatory identity verification and risk assessment process crucial for customer onboarding and ongoing Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance risk monitoring. This process is fundamental to organizational compliance policies.

## Purpose and Process

KYC is performed at the initial customer onboarding stage to verify identity and assess compliance risk. It is also subject to periodic refreshes thereafter. For non-individual customers, specific compliance mandates must be met before an account can be activated, including:
1.  Verifying formal identity documents.
2.  Establishing beneficial ownership.

## Key Outcomes and Applications

A primary outcome of the KYC process is the assignment of a customer risk rating, such as *Standard* or *Enhanced*. This rating directly dictates the level of ongoing scrutiny and the extent of documentation required for the customer.

The information gathered and risk ratings assigned through KYC profiles are critical for various downstream processes:
*   **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Risk-Based Monitoring**: AML transaction monitoring heavily relies on risk-based thresholds calibrated against a customer's KYC profile and assigned risk rating. System alerts are triggered when transactional behaviors deviate from established norms, including suspicious volumes, structuring patterns, or flows inconsistent with the customer's profile.
*   **Onboarding Risk Metrics**: KYC identity verification checks are a standard initial step in customer onboarding workflows, preceding credit risk assessment. While KYC establishes identity and compliance risk, a subsequent credit risk assessment quantifies the applicant's likelihood of default, influencing automated approval decisions and pricing parameters like interest rates and credit limits.
