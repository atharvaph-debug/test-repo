# Net Interest Margin Overview

Net Interest Margin (NIM), also known by its alias `nim`, is a key financial performance metric used to measure balance-sheet profitability. It quantifies the net interest margin generated per dollar of interest-earning assets.

## Key Features

Net Interest Margin is utilized as the primary metric for measuring balance-sheet profitability. It expresses how much margin is produced per dollar of interest-earning assets by quantifying the difference between interest earned on assets and interest paid on liabilities relative to total earning assets.

## Calculation

The Net Interest Margin is calculated using the following formula:

$$ \text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Net Interest Income (Interest Earned on Assets } - \text{ Interest Paid on Liabilities)}}{\text{Average Interest-Earning Assets}} $$

Alternatively, this can be expressed as:

$$ \text{NIM} = \frac{\text{Interest Income} - \text{Interest Expense}}{\text{Average Earning Assets}} $$
