# Customer Lifetime Value Overview

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total net profit expected from a customer over the entire span of their relationship. It is a key financial performance metric used to understand the long-term value of a customer to the business.

## Definition and Calculation
CLV is calculated as the projected customer revenue minus the cost to serve and retain that customer across their expected lifetime. The formula is expressed as:
$$\text{CLV} = \text{Projected Customer Revenue} - \text{Cost to Serve and Retain (across expected lifetime)}$$

## Aliases and Terminological Alignment
While officially referred to as "Customer Lifetime Value" (CLV) within corporate finance, the Growth marketing team often uses the term "customer LTV" to refer to this exact same metric. For metadata cataloging purposes, "customer LTV" and "CLV" are mapped as exact synonyms, acknowledging this terminological discrepancy between different divisions.
