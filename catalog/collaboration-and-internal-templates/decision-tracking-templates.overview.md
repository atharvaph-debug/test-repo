# Decision Tracking Templates Overview

Decision Tracking Templates are predefined schemas designed to register contextual backgrounds, proposed directions, and outcome ownership during team alignment phases. They standardize the metadata used for recording critical team decisions, the alternatives that were considered, and any resulting action items.

## Key Features

These templates enforce a structured approach to decision documentation, standardizing metadata for comprehensive tracking. Key aspects include:

*   **Decision Metadata**: Standardizes the recording of team decisions, the alternatives considered during the decision-making process, and the action items that result from these decisions.
*   **Mandatory Background Section**: Includes a required `Background` section specifically for documenting system constraints or challenges that influence the decision.
*   **Mandatory Relevant Data Section**: Features a compulsory `Relevant data` section, designed to contextualize decisions by providing relevant performance data or feedback.
