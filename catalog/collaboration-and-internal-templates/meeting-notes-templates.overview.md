# Meeting Notes Templates Overview

Meeting Notes Templates provide a consistent structural format designed to standardize the capture and organization of meeting metadata. These templates help in structuring agendas, active tracking lists, attendance records, discussion times, and immediate action items, thereby enriching the metadata associated with collaborative sessions.

## Key Features

This template offers a structured approach to capturing comprehensive meeting metadata, including:
*   **Dates:** The specific date(s) when the meeting took place.
*   **Participants:** Attendees of the meeting.
*   **Goals:** The objectives or aims of the meeting.
*   **Brainstorms:** Ideas or concepts generated during the discussion.
*   **Action Items:** Tasks or follow-ups identified during the meeting.
*   **Attendance Records:** Documenting who was present.
*   **Discussion Times:** Tracking the duration or timing of various discussion points.

## Structured Content

The template further organizes discussion topics into a structured table format. Each entry in this table includes:
*   **Time:** The specific time allocated or spent on a topic.
*   **Topic:** The subject of discussion.
*   **Presenter:** The individual leading the discussion for that topic, typically utilizing `@ mention` tags for easy identification.
*   **Notes:** Details and outcomes of the discussion.
