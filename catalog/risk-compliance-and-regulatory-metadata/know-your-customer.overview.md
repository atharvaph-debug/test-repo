# Know Your Customer Overview

Know Your Customer (KYC), also known by its aliases kyc and kyc-verification, is a fundamental process within financial crime compliance. It involves verifying customer identities, identifying beneficial ownership, and assessing the risk levels associated with customers. This process is initiated during customer onboarding and is refreshed at scheduled periodic intervals to ensure ongoing compliance.

## Key Features

*   **Purpose and Scope**: KYC serves to verify a customer's identity and to assess the associated risk they introduce to the business. It validates applicant identities using acceptable legal documentation. For business entities, KYC metadata must explicitly identify the beneficial owners.
*   **Lifecycle**: KYC verification begins at the point of customer onboarding. Accounts cannot be activated until this verification process is complete. It is then refreshed at scheduled periodic intervals.
*   **Compliance Pillar**: KYC is one of the three core operational pillars of Financial Crime Compliance, alongside Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring and Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR).
*   **Relationship with AML**: The baseline profiles established during KYC onboarding are crucial for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring. Transaction monitoring systems utilize these profiles within a risk-based framework to evaluate deviations in real-world customer activity, such as unusual transaction volumes or anomalous fund movements.
*   **Onboarding Context**: As part of the onboarding process for credit products, credit risk scores are programmatically fetched and appended to the applicant's profile, complementing the information gathered during KYC verification.
