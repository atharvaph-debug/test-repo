# Credit Risk Score Overview

The Credit Risk Score is a metric that quantifies the probability of a borrower failing to meet their repayment obligations. It is derived dynamically and used to assess individual borrower creditworthiness.

## Key Features

*   **Definition**: Credit Risk is defined as the probability that a borrower will fail to meet their repayment obligations. The Credit Risk Score quantifies this risk at the individual borrower level.
*   **Derivation**: This score is derived using three primary inputs:
    *   Repayment history
    *   Current exposure
    *   Income/leverage ratios

## Usage Contexts

*   **Risk Management**: The Credit Risk Score serves as a primary metric, alongside the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), to maintain organizational risk within acceptable boundaries.
*   **Customer Onboarding**: For credit product applications, the Credit Risk Score is programmatically fetched and appended to the applicant's profile as part of the onboarding request process.
