# Subscriber Churn Overview

Subscriber Churn, also known as attrition, represents the percentage of a telecommunications subscriber base that deactivates or cancels their service within a defined period, typically a quarter. This metric is a key indicator of customer retention and is influenced by various factors such as pricing, network performance, and competitive market dynamics.

## Definition

Churn is precisely defined as the percentage of the subscriber base that deactivates or cancels service during a given quarter, as detailed in the [Copy of Churn Analysis Quarterly Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15XrKEZF7PSXKCzdRbRj7BxleBcFn2onBv1QAkvDDlgs/edit) and [Copy of Customer Care Handbook](1mGNA1aQHvYEJS6N1cmr5N3nkTkZ8fX7Hjz-IvFOd_3o).

## Primary Drivers

Key factors contributing to subscriber churn include:
*   **Price:** A significant driver, particularly concentrated among single-product, out-of-contract subscribers, as noted in the [Copy of Churn Analysis Quarterly Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15XrKEZF7PSXKCzdRbRj7BxleBcFn2onBv1QAkvDDlgs/edit).
*   **Competitor Offers:** Aggressive port-in incentives from rival carriers can lead subscribers to switch providers ([Copy of Churn Analysis Quarterly Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15XrKEZF7PSXKCzdRbRj7BxleBcFn2onBv1QAkvDDlgs/edit)).
*   **Network Quality:** Poor coverage or inadequate VoLTE quality directly degrades customer experience, often resulting in subsequent service cancellations ([Copy of Churn Analysis Quarterly Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15XrKEZF7PSXKCzdRbRj7BxleBcFn2onBv1QAkvDDlgs/edit)).

## Retention Strategies

To mitigate churn, frontline care agents are instructed to identify the reasons for a subscriber's intent to leave and to apply approved retention options before processing any cancellations. Strategic efforts also involve targeting out-of-contract, single-product subscribers to safeguard high-value customer lifetime value (CLTV) segments ([Copy of Churn Analysis Quarterly Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15XrKEZF7PSXKCzdRbRj7BxleBcFn2onBv1QAkvDDlgs/edit), [Copy of Customer Care Handbook](1mGNA1aQHvYEJS6N1cmr5N3nkTkZ8fX7Hjz-IvFOd_3o)).
