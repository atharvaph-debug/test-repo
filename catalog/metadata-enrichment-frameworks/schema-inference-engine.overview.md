# Schema Inference Engine Overview

The Schema Inference Engine, also known by its aliases `sie` or `schema-inferer`, is a component designed to enrich metadata by automatically detecting and documenting structural schemas. As a key part of Metadata Enrichment Frameworks, it plays a crucial role in understanding and organizing data.

This engine specifically focuses on inferring schemas from unstructured or semi-structured datasets, providing valuable structural information where explicit schema definitions may be absent.
