# Suspicious Activity Report Overview

A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR), also known as `sar` or `sar-generation`, is a confidential, time-bound legal filing escalated by compliance investigators when transaction monitoring reveals suspicious behaviors.

## Role in the Compliance Pipeline

The SAR is the final step in the **Compliance Pipeline (KYC → AML → SAR)**. This process begins with **KYC (Know Your Customer)**, where identity verification is completed before a customer account is activated, and a KYC risk rating is established. This KYC risk rating serves as the metadata foundation for subsequent monitoring. **AML (Anti-Money Laundering) Monitoring** then scrutinizes transactions using a risk-based model, adjusting its intensity based on the customer's KYC profile and risk rating. If AML alerts indicate behavior inconsistent with the customer's KYC profile, investigators escalate these findings to a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR).

## Key Metadata Characteristics

SAR metadata is characterized by its high sensitivity, time-bound nature, and strict confidentiality, adhering to a "no tipping off" policy.
