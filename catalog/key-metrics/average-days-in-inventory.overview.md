# Average Days in Inventory Overview

Average Days in Inventory is a key metric that quantifies the average number of days a product unit remains in stock before being sold. This metric helps to understand inventory turnover efficiency.

## Key Features

This metric is calculated as the mean of the duration an inventory item spends in stock. Specifically, it measures the average of `(sold_at - created_at)` over all sold inventory items. It indicates how long a unit sits in stock from its creation (or entry into inventory) until it is sold.
