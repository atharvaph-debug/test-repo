# Office Facilities and Workplace Access Overview

The "Office Facilities and Workplace Access" policy establishes corporate rules governing active badges, building access clearance, and workplace attendance setups. It also covers guidelines for building badge access, visitor registration, and shared workspace etiquette.

## Key Features

This policy provides corporate guidelines for:
*   Building badge access
*   Visitor registration
*   Shared workspace etiquette

## Metadata Exclusions

For metadata enrichment purposes, it is important to note that this policy is entirely separate from warehouse or distribution center operations.
