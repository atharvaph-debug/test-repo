# Travel and Expense Compliance Overview

Travel and Expense Compliance outlines the policy rules, approved booking channels, reimbursement limits, and approval workflows managed by Finance Shared Services. These policies function as structured operational metadata, serving as reference points for knowledge base enrichment and metadata-as-code automation.

## Policy Ownership
The Travel and Expense policy is owned and maintained by Finance Shared Services.

## Booking and Travel Channels
*   **Booking Channels:** Flights and hotels must be booked through the approved corporate travel tool. Reimbursement for out-of-tool bookings requires documented prior manager approval.
*   **Travel Class Restrictions:** Economy class is mandatory for flights under six hours. Premium economy is reserved for longer flights and requires director-level sign-off.

## Reimbursement Guidelines
*   **Hotel Stays:** Must adhere to published per-city nightly caps.
*   **Meal Allowances:** Daily meal allowances must follow published per-diem guidelines specific to each city.
*   **Ground Transport:** Limited to standard rideshares or taxis; luxury tiers are not reimbursable.
*   **Personal-Vehicle Mileage:** Reimbursed at standard published rates.

## Deadlines and Auditing
*   **Submission Deadlines:** Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the spend date. Claims older than 60 days require an exception approval.
*   **Receipt Requirements:** Itemized receipts are required for any expense exceeding the established receipt threshold.
*   **Corporate Cards:** Personal expenses are strictly prohibited on corporate cards. Any mixed personal/corporate transactions must be immediately flagged and reimbursed to the company.
*   **Client Entertainment:** Claims for client entertainment require a documented business purpose and a full attendee list.

## Approval Workflows
Expense reports route to the claimant's manager for initial approval. Reports that contain policy exceptions require secondary approval routing to Finance Shared Services.
