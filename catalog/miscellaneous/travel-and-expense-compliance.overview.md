# Travel and Expense Compliance Overview

Travel and Expense Compliance defines the rules and policies governing eligible business travel planning processes and reimbursement categories. This policy specifically addresses how employees should book business travel and claim reimbursable expenses.

## Scope

This operational policy governs policies for booking business travel and claiming reimbursable expenses. It outlines the procedures and criteria for what constitutes eligible expenses and how they should be processed.

## Metadata Exclusions

This operational policy has no bearing on customer operations, risk operations, or treasury operations.
