# Number Portability Overview

Number Portability refers to the ability of a subscriber to retain their existing telephone number when switching between different carriers. This process is a key aspect of telecommunications, particularly concerning customer retention.

## Aliases and Synonyms

Depending on the system, department, or external industry standards, Number Portability is also referred to by several other terms:
*   "porting"
*   "Local Number Portability" (LNP)
*   "Mobile Number Portability" (MNP)

## Related Concepts

Number portability is discussed within the context of customer retention and churn analysis. Customer churn is defined as the rate at which subscribers leave or deactivate from a service over a specified period, and is often measured as a percentage share of the subscriber base that deactivated quarterly. The ability to retain a number (number portability) can influence customer decisions to switch carriers.
