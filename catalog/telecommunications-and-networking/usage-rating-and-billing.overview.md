# Usage Rating and Billing Overview

Usage Rating and Billing describes the operational sequence of applying pricing plans to individual usage events and aggregating them into recurring customer invoices. This process involves two key components: Rating and Billing.

## Key Concepts

*   **Rating**: This is the process of applying a customer's specific price plan to individual usage events. Its purpose is to calculate the corresponding charges for each event.
*   **Billing**: Following the rating process, billing involves the accumulation of these rated charges over the customer's recurring billing cycle. An invoice is generated immediately upon the close of this cycle, summarizing all accumulated charges.
