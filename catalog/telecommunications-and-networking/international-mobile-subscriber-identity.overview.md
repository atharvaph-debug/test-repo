# International Mobile Subscriber Identity Overview

The International Mobile Subscriber Identity (IMSI), also known as IMSI, is a globally unique identifier for a mobile subscriber. It is provisioned onto physical SIM cards and serves as the primary key for subscriber authentication and access, both for domestic connections and international roaming.

## Key Characteristics and Role

The IMSI plays a core role in telecommunications networks:
*   **Globally Unique Identifier**: It provides a distinct identity for each mobile subscriber worldwide.
*   **Provisioning and Registration**: The IMSI is programmed onto the SIM during the personalization phase and subsequently registered in the subscriber database, such as the Home Subscriber Server (HSS) or Home Location Register (HLR).
*   **Authentication and Access**: It acts as a secure "passport," authenticating the subscriber's identity with their home network. This enables visited networks to verify access when a subscriber is roaming, leveraging IMSI authentication mechanisms as part of bilateral roaming agreements between operators. The IMSI serves as the master key reference for both domestic connections and international roaming.
