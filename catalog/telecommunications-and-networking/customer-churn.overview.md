# Customer Churn Overview

Customer Churn, also known as customer attrition, is defined as the rate at which subscribers deactivate or leave a service over a specified period. This metric is crucial for understanding subscriber retention and overall business health.

## Definition and Measurement

Customer churn refers to the rate at which subscribers leave or deactivate from a service. It is measured and reported as the percentage share of the subscriber base that deactivated during a given quarter.

## Aliases

This concept is used interchangeably with "customer attrition."
